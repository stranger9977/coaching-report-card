The coaching WAR tab

How many wins is a head coach worth?

One number per coach: wins per season above what a fresh hire would have delivered, after the payroll he was handed, last season's quarterback and the franchise he works in. The uncertainty is the story.

Over 2012-2025 the head coaches who won the most above their resources are Belichick (+1.13), Arians (+1.08), McDermott (+1.08), Vrabel (+1.01) and Payton (+0.95). The bottom is Dennis Allen (-0.36), Lovie Smith (-0.30), Marrone, Bradley and McCoy. That order is what a fan would guess. The honest part: Belichick's interval runs from -0.21 to +2.47, nobody's lower bound clears zero, the number does not predict next season better than last season's win total does, and a coach cannot be cleanly separated from his quarterback. This measures who won above their resources. It cannot prove the man, rather than the man plus quarterback plus building, is why.

How it is built

Wins above a first-season hire

Expected wins. The closing point spreads on a team's games imply the wins the betting market expected. The part of that expectation explained by payroll (top 25 contracts as a share of the cap), the starting quarterback's cap share, and his EPA per dropback the season before is called talent. It explains 36% of the market's expectation. Actual wins minus talent is wins above talent, scaled to 17 games.

The coach's share. A mixed model of wins above talent with three random terms: coach, franchise, season. The franchise term absorbs the owner, the front office and whatever outlasts the man. The coach term is shrunk toward zero, hardest for short careers.

Replacement level is measured, not assumed: what week-1 first-season head coaches actually delivered, net of franchise and season, on 99 such seasons. It comes out at -0.19 wins against the average sitting coach (SE 0.25). Interims are excluded (they average -1.20). Because the line is near zero, WAR and wins above average are almost the same thing here.

What is and is not controlled. Payroll, the quarterback's pay and his previous season, the building. Not controlled: roster quality beyond payroll, and the quarterback's play in the same season. That second one is the biggest hole. Same-season QB play explains 39% of wins above talent on its own, and the published coach effect correlates 0.58 with the QB play each coach had. Put it in as a control and 24 of 50 coaches move 10 or more places (Jeff Fisher 45th to 10th, Andy Reid 11th to 39th). The table carries that rank beside the main one.

Madden. Adding Madden ratings to the talent control, where they exist (2017 onward), leaves the board's order 98% intact: McDermott 1st, Vrabel 2nd, Arians 3rd, McVay 4th, Belichick 5th (+0.87), John Harbaugh the one real mover (8th to 17th). Madden does catch roster quality that payroll misses (its leftover signal drops from +0.68 wins per standard deviation to +0.07), but a control that switches on mid-window grades the same coach on two scales, so it is a sensitivity, not the main model.

Active head coaches

The 32 coaches on a sideline this season

Same numbers, filtered to the 2026 week-1 head coaches. Vrabel leads at +1.01, then Payton, Sirianni, John Harbaugh, McVay. Fourteen of the 32 have under four seasons in the window, so their number is mostly the prior; six have never been a head coach in it.

By stint, because the model says the effect is a pairing. The main number pools every season a coach has had, so Vrabel's seven seasons are six in Tennessee and one in New England, Payton's twelve are nine in New Orleans and three in Denver. Split by team and shrunk with the model's own prior (about four seasons' worth): Vrabel was +0.46 a season in Tennessee and his one New England season, raw +6.9, shrinks to +1.30. Payton was +0.62 in New Orleans, +0.97 in Denver. Reid's one Philadelphia season in the window was -6.2 raw; Kansas City is +1.58 over thirteen. Jim Harbaugh: +0.57 in San Francisco, +0.72 with the Chargers. McCarthy: +0.03 in Green Bay, +0.49 in Dallas. Bowles: -0.78 with the Jets, +0.15 in Tampa. Inside one stint the coach and the building cannot be told apart, so the franchise term is not subtracted here.

What the skeptics tried to break

Three rounds, forty objections, and the board did not move

Three reviewers, each with a different job (survivorship, statistical practice, confounds), took three passes at it. Most objections were right. The fixes were made, the top of the board stayed put, and every claim around it got narrower. What survived, and what did not:

Nobody is clear of replacement. The first bootstrap was too tight. Redone properly, P(above average) for the top five is 0.88 to 0.92, nobody reaches 0.95, and Belichick's 90% rank range is 1 to 31.

The number is not yet reliable. The coach rank across odd and even seasons correlates 0.08, under the project's own 0.30 gate. The coach spread that sets the scale has a 95% interval from 0.00 to 1.32 wins. Held at the 0.40 the reliability supports, Belichick is +0.50.

It does not predict. Fit on earlier seasons, WAR predicts the next season's wins above talent at r = 0.13; last season's raw win total does it at r = 0.21, and WAR adds nothing once that is in the model (p = 0.50).

It is a pairing, not a man. The part of a coach's effect that travels with him across teams is 0.01 wins; the coach-within-team part is 1.26. Belichick without his elite-quarterback seasons is +0.28; Tomlin +0.41; Arians +0.74. Twelve of 50 coaches move more than 0.3 wins on a single season (Vrabel without 2025: +0.67).

Tenure cannot be called earned. Coaches who lasted 5+ seasons were +3.5 wins better in years 1-2 than coaches gone within two. A simulated league with no coaching skill and owners who fire on wins produces most of that gap (median +2.2), so the claim was withdrawn.

Replacement level is soft. First-time head coaches average +0.04, second-job coaches -0.64 (p = 0.26); the line drifts 0.54 wins between halves of the window (p = 0.37). Neither moves anyone in the top 12.

Cheap quarterbacks count for the coach. 45 of 451 coach-seasons have a starter paid below average and playing above it; they average +2.8 wins above talent, and 10 of the top 12 coaches have at least one. Without them Vrabel is +0.54 and McVay +0.53.
